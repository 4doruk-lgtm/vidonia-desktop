FAQ

Questions, answered.

Is Vidonia free?

Yes. Both modes are free to use.

What is the difference between Recommended and Aggressive?

Recommended applies a stable, balanced enhancement that keeps your video's look. Aggressive applies stronger sharpening and detail recovery. It takes longer and can cause lag on long clips.

Are my videos stored?

Uploads are processed on our server and deleted after processing. Results are removed about an hour after they are created.

Do I need an account?

No. Just upload a video, choose a mode and download the result.

Why can the first request be slow?

The free server sleeps when unused. The first request can take up to a minute to wake it up.
