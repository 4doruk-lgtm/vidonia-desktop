Questions

Short answers.

Is it free?

Yes. Every mode is free, and there is no sign-up.

Are my videos stored?

Uploads are processed and removed afterwards. Results are deleted about an hour after they are created.

What is the difference between the modes?

Recommended is balanced and stable. Aggressive pushes clarity harder and takes longer. TikTok Ready makes a vertical 9:16 file with a higher bitrate.

Why can the first request be slow?

The free server sleeps when it is not used. The first request can take up to a minute to wake it up.
